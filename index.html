The heart behind the message

Freedom is possible. The journey can begin today.

Offense can take root quietly, bitterness can grow over time, and painful experiences can shape the way we see ourselves and others. I Refuse to Stay Bound invites readers to bring those places before Jesus Christ, examine what has taken hold, and pursue a life marked by forgiveness, wisdom, truth, accountability, and spiritual freedom.

This is a call to look honestly at our own hearts while refusing to excuse wrongdoing or confuse forgiveness with the absence of boundaries. Biblical truth calls us to both compassion and responsibility as we seek to walk in the freedom of Jesus Christ.
