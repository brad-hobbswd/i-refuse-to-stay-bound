Privacy Policy

How this website handles information when you visit or get in touch.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

About this policy

This policy applies to the I Refuse to Stay Bound website maintained by Bradley Hobbs. It explains the general information practices for this website. The site is intended to introduce the book and author and provide a way to make contact.

Information you provide

If you email Bradley using the email link on this website, you choose what information to include in your message. That information is used to read and respond to your inquiry and to follow up where reasonably necessary. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

Information collected by the hosting provider

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub and its service providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses, browser details, and request logs, as part of providing, securing, and maintaining the hosting service. Those practices are governed by GitHub’s own policies and settings. This website does not itself promise that hosting-related logs are disabled.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally operate its own advertising or analytics program through the site code. However, the hosting platform or third-party services you use to access the site may employ technical storage or logging. Your browser settings can help you manage cookies and related technologies.

Sharing and retention

Information you send directly by email is not sold by this website. It may be processed by the email provider and may be disclosed where required by law or needed to protect rights and security. Messages are retained only as reasonably needed to respond, maintain relevant correspondence, or meet applicable obligations.

External links

This website may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by those providers, not by this site. Review their policies before sharing information with them.

Your choices and questions

You may choose not to contact the site or to limit the information you include in a message. For a privacy question or a request regarding information you have sent directly, contact brad.hobbs13@icloud.com.

This policy is a general description of current intended practices, not legal advice. It should be reviewed if the website adds forms, newsletters, analytics, advertising, or other data collection features.
