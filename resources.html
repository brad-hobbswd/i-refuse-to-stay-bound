Resources

The primary resource is the book itself, with each chapter bringing its message and practical response together.

Everything belongs with the chapter

I Refuse to Stay Bound brings its teaching, reflection, affirmations, and prayers into the 27 chapters. The prayers and affirmations, along with the teaching and reflections, are written into the chapters themselves. They are not separate downloadable materials or a second collection that repeats the manuscript; they are part of the writing and the intended reading experience.

Teaching within the chapters

Follow the chapter’s message as it develops, connecting its central teaching with the larger journey toward freedom from offense and bitterness.

Reflection and application

Pause with the reflection included in the chapter and consider how its message can be lived out with honesty, grace, and wisdom.

Affirmations

Use the affirmations within each chapter to revisit its message and keep its truth in view as you continue reading.

Prayer

Make room for the chapter’s prayer as a personal response to Jesus Christ and an opportunity to bring the matters it addresses before Him.
