Explore the Written Chapters

I Refuse to Stay Bound explores the journey from betrayal, offense, and bitterness toward forgiveness, healing, accountability, and spiritual freedom. Each chapter develops the book's central message through biblical teaching, personal reflection, practical application, group discussion, and prayer.

Use the chapter index below to find a subject and jump directly to its overview on this page. If you are reading the book, use the chapter number to locate the corresponding chapter in your book. If you are leading a group study, the chapter themes can help you identify a topic for discussion.

Using a Chapter for Personal or Group Study

  • Read the chapter in full before discussing its questions.
  • Look up the key Scriptures in context and reflect on how they inform the chapter's message.
  • Use the included group-study questions to encourage thoughtful conversation without pressuring anyone to disclose personal details.
  • Complete the personal reflection and practical application at a pace that respects each person's circumstances.
  • Close with the chapter's prayer, allowing room for continued growth, appropriate support, and responsible next steps.

These chapter resources are intended to encourage spiritual growth and honest reflection. They do not replace professional support, safeguarding procedures, or appropriate reporting when serious harm or danger is involved.
