The author

Bradley Hobbs

Writing to encourage honest reflection, deeper faith, and a life that keeps moving toward the freedom found in Jesus Christ.

A message shaped around freedom

Bradley Hobbs is the author of I Refuse to Stay Bound, a Christian book in development about overcoming offense, releasing bitterness, and pursuing spiritual freedom through Jesus Christ.

Through this project, Bradley hopes to offer readers thoughtful teaching and practical resources that invite them to reflect honestly, engage with Scripture, pray, and take meaningful steps toward spiritual growth.

This author biography is an initial website draft. It can be expanded with Bradley’s preferred personal story, ministry background, previous publications, and speaking information.
