The author

Bradley Hobbs

Writing to encourage honest reflection, deeper faith, and a life that keeps moving toward the freedom found in Jesus Christ.

A message shaped around freedom

Bradley Hobbs writes about Christian faith, forgiveness, spiritual growth, and the difficult work of moving beyond offense and bitterness. In I Refuse to Stay Bound, he invites readers to examine what they carry, bring their struggles before Jesus Christ, and pursue freedom through biblical truth, prayer, reflection, and practical application.

His hope is that readers will find encouragement to face painful experiences honestly, grow in discernment, and keep moving toward a life shaped by forgiveness, wisdom, and faith.
