Inside the Chapters

The message and the moments of personal application are woven into the book itself.

One complete journey through 27 chapters

I Refuse to Stay Bound is being developed as a connected journey toward overcoming offense, releasing bitterness, and walking in the freedom of Jesus Christ. The teaching, Scripture-based reflection, prayers, affirmations, and practical application are all written directly into the chapters themselves, not published as separate companion studies.

Each part belongs with the chapter it was written to support. Readers can work through the material personally, pause for prayer and reflection, or use the chapter as a starting point for meaningful conversation in a church or small-group setting.

How to use the book

  • Read each chapter thoughtfully and allow its message to settle before moving forward.
  • Spend time with the reflection and application included in the chapter.
  • Engage with the prayer and affirmations already written into the chapter as part of your personal time with Jesus Christ.
  • For a group discussion, invite participants to share what they learned and how they can apply it with honesty, wisdom, and grace.
About the chapter list: The book is planned as 27 chapters. Their individual titles and content will be presented as the manuscript and publication details are finalized. All prayers and affirmations are already included in the chapter writing; no separate or duplicate study-guide series is planned.
