Affirmations

I can acknowledge my pain without allowing it to define my future.

With the help of Jesus Christ, I can choose forgiveness while practicing wisdom and healthy boundaries.

My past may be part of my story, but it does not have to govern every step ahead.

Reflections

  • What hurt am I still carrying?
  • How has this experience shaped the way I respond to others?
  • What is one faithful, wise step I can take toward freedom?
