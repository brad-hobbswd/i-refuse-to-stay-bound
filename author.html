The author behind the message

Bradley Hobbs

A writer, educator, and student of Scripture seeking to encourage honest reflection, deeper faith, and a life shaped by the freedom found in Jesus Christ.

Faith, truth, and spiritual freedom

Writing from a desire to see people walk in freedom

Bradley Hobbs writes about Christian faith, forgiveness, spiritual growth, and the difficult work of moving beyond offense and bitterness. His writing invites readers to look honestly at the places where pain has taken root, bring those places before Jesus Christ, and allow biblical truth to shape their next steps.

In I Refuse to Stay Bound, Bradley addresses the realities of betrayal, unresolved hurt, forgiveness, discernment, accountability, and healthy boundaries. His aim is not to offer easy answers or to minimize painful experiences, but to encourage readers to seek God sincerely and pursue a freedom that changes how they live, love, and respond.

The inspiration behind this book

Bradley recognizes the impact that John Bevere’s The Bait of Satan has had on Christian conversations about offense. That work helped bring attention to a subject that many believers need to examine. I Refuse to Stay Bound is Bradley’s own original work, developed in his own voice and through his own approach to teaching, reflection, prayer, and personal application.

The message is intended to help readers take an honest look at their own hearts without confusing forgiveness with the absence of accountability or assuming that healing requires ignoring harm. The journey calls for prayer, truth, wisdom, and a willingness to let Jesus Christ lead the process.

Why this message matters

Bradley hopes this book will serve individuals, church groups, and Bible study communities that are ready to move beyond simply discussing offense and begin applying Scripture to their own lives. The goal is not to keep readers focused on what someone else has done, but to invite each person to examine their own response, seek God’s direction, and take faithful steps toward freedom.
