A little about my journey

Writing with faith, purpose, and a heart for people

Bradley Hobbs is a Christian author and educator whose interests bring together Scripture, teaching, communication, and service. His work is rooted in a continuing desire to learn, grow in faith, and encourage people to consider how biblical truth can shape everyday choices.

With a degree in Computer Information Technology and Business Management and professional experience in early childhood education, Bradley has seen how patience, consistency, clear communication, and thoughtful guidance can help people grow. Those same values inform his approach to writing: take time with the subject, look carefully at what Scripture teaches, and make room for honest reflection and practical application.

Writing is one way Bradley shares what he is learning and invites others to keep growing. His aim is not to present himself as someone who has every answer, but to point readers toward Jesus Christ and the wisdom of God's Word.

Faith that continues to grow

Bradley's Christian life and involvement in the church have helped shape his interest in teaching that reaches beyond information and encourages real spiritual growth. He believes Scripture deserves careful attention and that faith should influence the way we treat others, respond to difficulty, accept correction, and make decisions.

That conviction is part of what draws him to devotional writing and Bible based teaching. He hopes his work encourages readers to pray, study the Word, examine their own hearts, and seek the Lord with sincerity rather than settle for appearances or easy answers.

A few parts of Bradley's work

Christian writing

Developing books, devotionals, and teaching material that invite readers to reflect on Scripture and apply it to daily life.

Education and service

Supporting learning and development through experience in early childhood education and work with children and families.

Scripture and spiritual growth

Continuing to study biblical teaching and explore how truth can be lived with conviction, humility, wisdom, and love.

Technology and communication

Using digital tools and web design to organize information and make educational and faith based resources easier to access.

Why I write

Bradley writes because words can open the door to reflection, bring difficult questions into the light, and help people take a thoughtful next step. He is especially interested in material that connects the message of Scripture with the realities people face in relationships, church life, personal growth, and the process of becoming more like Christ.

He hopes readers will approach each resource with an open Bible and a willing heart, testing what they read against Scripture and taking time to pray and consider its application. The goal is not simply to finish a book, but to keep growing in truth and in the way we live out our faith.

The book behind this page

I Refuse to Stay Bound grew from a desire to explore how believers can respond to offense and hurt without allowing bitterness to shape the rest of their lives. Its teaching considers forgiveness alongside truth, accountability, discernment, and healthy boundaries. The book page explains the message in greater detail, while the chapter and resource pages offer ways to continue exploring the material.

Thank you for taking a moment to learn more about the person behind the writing. I hope what you find here encourages you to keep seeking Jesus Christ and to let His Word guide your walk.
